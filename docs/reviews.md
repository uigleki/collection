# Reviews

Brief impressions designed to aid memory and recognition
while avoiding spoilers.

## 📺 Anime

### 化物語

Supernatural tales of adolescent awakening

- **Dialogue as art**: Rapid-fire wordplay
  and philosophy layered through every exchange.
  Humor through tragedy, meaning created in the interplay itself.
- **Visual storytelling reimagined**: Shinbo and Shaft transcending animation.
  Text flashing, impossible angles, empty cities.
  Color expressing psychology, art styles transforming mid-scene.
- **Aberrations as self-confrontation**:
  Supernatural curses externalizing inner wounds.
  Each girl must confront her own burden.
  Only you can save yourself, though others can walk beside you.

### 偽物語

When fake becomes more genuine than real

- **Continued Shinbo/Shaft mastery**: Same visual transcendence,
  dialogue artistry, aberrations as self-confrontation.

### ハイスコアガール

When words fail, fighting games speak

- **She never speaks, yet says everything**:
  Akira conveys more through her hands on the arcade stick
  and flickers in her eyes than dialogue ever could.
- **He alone understands her**: Everyone sees the perfect rich girl.
  Only Haruo reads what her silence truly means.
- **Fiercest rivals, unbreakable bond**: She destroys him at Street Fighter,
  yet competition births understanding rather than resentment.
- **OP and ED that demand presence**:
  So integral to the story's soul
  that skipping them feels like tearing pages from a book.

### 少女終末旅行

Finding beauty in a world that has ended

- **Wondering about everything**:
  Chito and Yuuri ask questions about the world with pure curiosity,
  turning simple discoveries into gentle reflections on life.
- **Two souls as one humanity**:
  Contemplative Chito and spirited Yuuri complete each other.
  Their bond proves connection matters more than purpose.
- **Beauty in the mundane**: Laundry days, impromptu baths,
  small joys discovered.
  Simplicity reveals what truly matters.

## 🎬 Movies

### 打ち上げ花火、下から見るか？横から見るか？

The summer that refuses to end

- **Shinbo's stream-of-consciousness mastery**:
  Reality and emotion interwoven beyond narrative logic.
  Visual language capturing how summer exists in memory rather than fact.
- **Ultimate youth fantasy realized**: The dream every teenager carries.
  One perfect day that never ends,
  escaping with the person who matters before they vanish forever.
- **Relentless pursuit of beautiful possibility**:
  Each loop reaching for what could be rather than accepting what is.
  Refusing to surrender perfect moments to inevitability.
- **Poetry made tangible**: Seaside, fireworks,
  fantasy fused beyond description.
  Beauty intensified by impermanence.

### ペンギン・ハイウェイ

Scientific wonder meets mysterious beauty

- **A child's earnest investigation**: Aoyama documents every penguin,
  tests hypotheses, approaches the impossible with genuine scientific devotion.
- **The Lady as pure mystery**: Beautiful, inexplicable, beyond understanding.
  Her presence needs no explanation.
- **Time spent together**: Summer days investigating,
  discovering the world side by side.
  The moments matter more than answers.

### Charlie and the Chocolate Factory

Where wonder meets weirdness

- **Visual wonderland realized**: Chocolate factory as dreamscape.
  Kaleidoscopic colors, rivers and landscapes that feel tangibly real.
- **Wonka's captivating strangeness**: Bizarre, unpredictable,
  constantly surprising.
  Child-like yet unsettling.
  Weirdness that somehow works.
- **Innocence rewarded**:
  Charlie stays pure while spoiled children meet delicious consequences.
  Goodness prevails through dark humor.
- **Love over indulgence**:
  Charlie's devotion to family versus others' selfishness.
  Difficult times pass when you have those who matter.

## 🎮 Games

### To the Moon

A memory journey embracing final moonlight vows

- **Reverse chronology reveals**:
  Deeply moving romance unveiled through fragmentary recollections.
  Effect shown before cause, making each revelation increasingly heartbreaking.
- **"For River" and everything after**:
  Theme song perfectly elevating emotional impact.
  Hauntingly melodic piano themes subtly remixed throughout,
  layering emotion upon emotion.
- **Symbolic imagery threading through**: Rabbits, moonlight, lighthouses.
  Simple mysteries concealing surprising, devastating revelations.

### What Remains of Edith Finch

Every room a theater of memory

- **Each tale through unique devices**:
  Every story presented through distinct narrative forms.
  Kaleidoscope of innovative approaches.
- **First-person fantastical journeys**:
  Experiencing impossible lives through your own eyes.
  Walking through others' moments as if your own.
- **Fantasy and reality fused**: No boundary between imagination and truth.
  Memory transformed into lived experience.

### Finding Paradise

A soul's journey toward perfect existence

- **Warm life contemplation**: Thoughtful examination of ideals, reality,
  and regret.
  What it means to pursue happiness and reconcile reality with fantasy.
- **The smallest moments matter most**:
  Story revealing mundane details as ultimately most significant.
  Characters flawed yet real, imagination woven with truth.
- **Mementos as prisms**: Each object imbued with myriad meanings.
  Viewing multi-faceted lifetimes through simple things,
  certain items creating profoundly moving moments.

### Steins;Gate

Fate's ultimate decree

- **Rigorous time travel mythology**:
  Complete and internally consistent scientific framework.
  Real-world science woven seamlessly into narrative.
- **Everything placed with purpose**:
  Masterful foreshadowing from opening to conclusion.
  Every detail meticulously positioned, all elements converging.
- **Salvation versus sacrifice**:
  Harrowing choices between saving one or another.
  The weight of altering fate bearing down on every decision.
- **Bonds across world lines**: Sincere connections deepening across timelines.
  Understanding forged through shared impossible experiences.

**Shortcomings**:

- **Slow initial pacing**:
  Extended slice-of-life foundation requiring patience through narrative setup.
- **Cryptic true ending requirements**:
  Key decision points designed with excessive subtlety,
  nearly demanding outside guidance.

### 7年後で待ってる

"I'll be waiting for you, 7 years from now"

- **Cross-temporal commitment**: A promise made seven years ago,
  crystallizing the most sincere feelings.
- **Story through recovered fragments**:
  Memory exploration gradually unveiling truth.
  Each chapter drawing you forward through carefully crafted revelations.
- **"LIFE" strikes profound resonance**: The resolution theme at the climax.
  When emotions truly hit.

**Shortcomings**:

- **Some forced narrative turns**:
  Excessive pursuit of suspense occasionally resulting in less organic plot
  developments.

### ASTLIBRA Revision

15 years to resurrect the golden age

- **Unpredictable narrative progression**:
  Altering the past through mystical scales produces dramatic,
  unexpected developments.
- **Outstanding musical experience**: Soundtrack perfectly complementing scenes,
  creating full immersion from combat to story.
- **Impactful climactic moments**:
  Key story beats filled with gravitas and emotional weight.
  Exploring fate, sacrifice, love, and the meaning of choices and regret.
- **Golden age RPG reborn**: Capturing pure joy of side-scrolling action JRPGs.
  Rekindling childhood wonder.

**Shortcomings**:

- **Grind-heavy with punishing difficulty**:
  Late-game requires substantial grinding
  and features punishing difficulty spikes.

## 🎨 Artists

### カントク

Master of moe aesthetics and light

- **Masterful light and shadow**:
  Strong compositional ability with exquisite lighting effects.
  Technical precision in every piece.
- **Moe perfected**: Vibrant colors, lively expressions, meticulous detail.
  Characters leap from the page through carefully drawn faces and emotions.
- **Unwavering consistency**: Every illustration maintains exceptional quality.
  Colors balanced, lines stable, anatomy precise.
  Professional reliability across all works.
